Biography

I am a PhD researcher at the IMDEA Software Institute and Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, advised by Thaleia Dimitra Doudali and Marco Guarnieri.

My research focuses on the security of Machine Learning Serving Systems. I study how performance optimizations in multi-tenant LLM serving, such as prefix caching and request batching, leak information between users, and how to build defenses that close these leaks without giving up efficiency.
